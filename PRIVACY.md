# FolderMoji Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026  
Developer: Paul Williams

## Summary

FolderMoji is designed to operate locally and privately. It does not include advertising or analytics, does not create a FolderMoji account, and does not send bookmark titles, URLs, browsing history, emoji selections, or colour choices to Paul Williams or an external artificial-intelligence service.

## Information FolderMoji accesses

FolderMoji requests Chrome's `bookmarks` permission so it can display and, when directed by the user, create, rename, move, update, open, or delete bookmarks and bookmark folders.

FolderMoji requests Chrome's `storage` permission to preserve:

- folder text-colour preferences;
- counts used to order private on-device emoji suggestions;
- restoration records identifying the former location of items placed in FolderMoji Trash; and
- other settings necessary to provide the extension's stated bookmark-organization functions.

## Storage and synchronization

Emoji characters placed in a bookmark or folder name become part of that Chrome bookmark title and may synchronize through the user's browser account when Chrome Sync is enabled.

Folder text-colour preferences use Chrome synchronized extension storage and may therefore be processed by the user's browser and account provider to synchronize the extension across supported desktop installations. Emoji-usage counts and Trash restoration records are stored locally in the extension's browser storage. Paul Williams does not receive either category of information.

Google and the user's operating system or browser provider may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies. FolderMoji does not control those services.

## Information not collected by the developer

FolderMoji does not intentionally collect or transmit to the developer:

- personally identifying information;
- authentication credentials;
- payment or financial information;
- bookmark titles or URLs;
- general browsing history;
- precise location;
- communications;
- health information;
- advertising identifiers; or
- analytics or behavioural profiles.

## Sharing and sale

FolderMoji does not sell user information. Because FolderMoji does not transmit the information described above to the developer, the developer does not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or artificial-intelligence providers.

## Data control and deletion

Users may rename or delete bookmarks through FolderMoji or Chrome. Users may remove FolderMoji through Chrome's extension settings. Browser providers may retain synchronized information, backups, or logs according to their own retention policies. Users should consult and manage their Chrome Sync settings separately.

## Children

FolderMoji is a general-purpose organizational tool and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through FolderMoji.

## Security

FolderMoji minimizes permissions and does not use a developer-operated server in this version. No software environment can be guaranteed absolutely secure; users should maintain browser and operating-system updates and appropriate backups of important bookmarks.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be identified by an updated effective date and included with the relevant FolderMoji release. Where required, the Chrome Web Store listing and privacy disclosures will also be updated.

## Contact

Privacy or permissions questions may be directed to the developer through the official FolderMoji Chrome Web Store support contact.

This policy should be hosted at a stable public URL before FolderMoji is submitted for public distribution.
